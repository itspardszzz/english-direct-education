Applicant Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common queries regarding schedules, payouts, and platforms.

When are peak teaching hours?

Peak hours are weekday evenings from 6:00 PM to 10:00 PM (Philippine Time), and weekend slots from 9:00 AM to 10:00 PM.

What is the payment schedule and rate?

The stated rate is ₱60 per completed 25-minute lesson, disbursed weekly directly to your bank account or verified e-wallet, subject to completed classes and company policies.

Do teachers need to make their own slide decks?

No. All lesson decks, vocabulary exercises, and reading materials are provided directly in ClassIn and VooV.

What is the expected review timeframe?

Our target review period is 24–48 hours. Shortlisted applicants are contacted to schedule a 15-minute simulated demo class.
